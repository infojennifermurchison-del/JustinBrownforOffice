About

About Justin Randall Brown

His Story

I didn't decide to serve because of politics — I decided to serve because I care deeply about my community.

Over the years, I've had the opportunity to work with residents, local businesses, seniors, and young people, and one thing has become clear: people want leadership that is present, listens, and follows through. Too many voices feel unheard, and too many opportunities are left on the table.

As a husband, father, and homeowner in East Orange's Second Ward, this isn't just where I live — it's where my family is building our future. The same streets my children grow up on are the streets I want to help make stronger, safer, and full of opportunity for every family.

That's why I'm running for City Council. I believe our community deserves leadership that empowers people, values accountability, serves with purpose, and leads with transparency. My goal isn't simply to hold office — it's to build trust, bring people together, and deliver meaningful results.

East Orange has given so much to my family. Now, I'm ready to give even more in return. Together, we can restore trust, deliver results, and build a stronger East Orange.

Family

Family has always been at the center of Justin's life. Raised by his mother and father and inspired by the values passed down by his grandparents, he learned early the importance of faith, hard work, integrity, and serving others. Today, Justin and his wife, Cindy, are proud homeowners in East Orange's Second Ward, where they are raising their two sons, Justin Randall II and Micah Jeremiah. Having called East Orange home for most of his life, Justin is deeply invested in the city's future and the families who call it home.

The Record

A Record of Service

Justin has dedicated his career to public service — in government, in the nonprofit sector, and in the neighborhoods he calls home. His experience has always been centered on helping people, solving problems, and strengthening the community.

City HallDeputy City Clerk

Former Deputy City Clerk

Reviewed and prepared resolutions, ordinances, and legislation for City Council; coordinated distribution and execution of the East Orange Municipal Budget; collaborated with the Mayor's Office, Governing Body, and municipal departments; maintained and attested official documents, appointments, and board/commission terms; processed OPRA requests; supported and certified municipal elections and voter registration; oversaw notary services and staff; and served as a liaison between residents, elected officials, and municipal departments.

Mayor's OfficeConstituent Services

Former Constituent Services Manager

Managed constituent services for the Mayor's Office, addressing resident concerns, complaints, and community issues; coordinated with City departments and community stakeholders to resolve constituent matters and connect residents with resources; planned community meetings, events, and outreach initiatives; recognized resident achievements and community milestones; and served as a liaison between residents, City leadership, and municipal departments.

NonprofitJusMotivated

Founder & President, JusMotivated

Led community initiatives supporting youth, families, entrepreneurs, and local businesses; connected small businesses with funding opportunities, resources, and increased community visibility; developed mentorship and leadership opportunities for youth and young entrepreneurs; and organized community events, outreach efforts, and family-focused programs that provide resources and strengthen community connections.

EducationPolitical Science
